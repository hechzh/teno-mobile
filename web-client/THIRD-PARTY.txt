The Web preview reuses Flutter and the shared Teno UI's Inter font.
Noto Sans SC is bundled under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
The regular-weight font is subset to Latin, general punctuation, CJK punctuation,
CJK Unified Ideographs and Extension A, and full-width forms; encoded as WOFF2.
License: assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSansSC.txt (also included in the built assets).
The original provider font is not a runtime CDN dependency.
