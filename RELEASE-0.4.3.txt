Teno Android 0.4.3 — Recording flow and Tasks
Release date: 2026-11-02 (Asia/Shanghai)
Package: com.teno.app / versionCode 8

Install dist/Teno-0.4.3.apk over 0.4.2. Keep the app installed to retain recordings and recorder keys.

Recording
- Record with device starts the authorized recorder directly. If disconnected, it reconnects the last successfully authorized recorder. First binding still needs service/key verification.
- Recording preferences are explained once and remembered. Automatic cloud processing and Wi-Fi-only uploads are optional. Live transcription has its own switch.
- Stop and save automatically keeps phone audio. Recorder audio is synced to the phone after the device confirms stop, with length/end-marker/CRC verification and decoding before upload.
- There is no separate Keep on phone or Save and continue step. A local copy remains after successful upload. Rename and local playback are optional actions on the saved recording.
- Recorder-button recordings sync when connected again. Interrupted transfers retry the original file; successfully synced device entries are skipped. Recorder originals are never deleted automatically.
- Automatic uploads are queued, survive interruption and resume on connectivity/app launch. Transient failures retry up to three times; format/authentication failures require action. Force-stopped apps must be reopened.
- Team sharing, speaker confirmation and publication remain explicit and unchanged.

Tasks
- Long personal task titles use their full measured height and no longer overlap their descriptions.
- Action buttons have gaps and stack at large font sizes. Team filters wrap whole labels instead of splitting words in narrow equal-width columns.

Boundaries
No physical JWD or Magic4 Pro acceptance is claimed. Test transport replies and loopback transcripts are not real BLE or Azure recognition. Existing JWD import limit is 60 minutes/100 MiB; general import is 120 minutes/250 MiB; phone capture is 54 minutes. New automatic flows need physical firmware/timing/lock-screen validation. No backend, website or production team data was changed.
Existing Summary/Transcript tabs in recording results can still break within words at 360dp/1.5 text scaling. This is outside the Tasks overlap correction; the screenshots do not certify the whole app's typography.

Recovery
Failed uploads keep the local file. Use Retry upload on the saved recording. A failed recorder sync keeps the original on the device; reconnect or use Device recordings to retry. No uninstall, reset, format or automatic deletion is required.
