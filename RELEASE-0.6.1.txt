IMPLEMENTATION
Home uses circular actions, a JWD capsule, Upcoming, date groups and a bottom Ask entry. Navigation shifts Home aside; the account replaces quota content.
Library uses expandable system collections and nested folders, real complete counts, updated times, right-side selection and a unified batch toolbar. Photos, Notes and On this phone remain under My files; Trash is in the more menu.
Search combines Folder, Type, Speaker and Date range, with explicit selection, sort direction and pagination. Unsupported new controls are hidden on old backends.
Recording/pause and import open from Home as real bottom sheets. Transcript playback, chapters, timecodes, current speaker names, text variants and context menus use shared controls.
Templates retain Teno's content in My templates, categories and category shelves. Favorites, recent and created templates live in the My templates subpage.
Calendar uses month/week/Today navigation and event cards grouped by local date. Calendar connection, ICS import and manual events are in its menu.
Sharing is a bottom panel with preview and a content child panel; fixed versions, expiry and revocation remain. Export starts with Audio/Transcript/Summary and then format/options. Ask contains Chat only; settings use grouped subpages.
No Work, Tasks, Skills, Routines, Connectors, payment, teams, third-party sign-in or voiceprint entries were added. No unusable placeholders.
